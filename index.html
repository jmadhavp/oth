How to Play Othello

🎯 Objective

Have the most pieces of your color when the board is full or no more moves are possible.

🎮 How to Play

  • Black always starts first
  • Place your piece to outflank opponent pieces (sandwich them between your pieces)
  • All opponent pieces caught between your new piece and existing pieces flip to your color
  • You must flip at least one opponent piece every turn
  • Valid moves are shown with glowing indicators
  • If no valid moves available, your turn is automatically passed

⏰ Timer Rules

  • Each player has 15 seconds per turn
  • Timer color: Green (15-10s) → Yellow (10-5s) → Red (5-0s)
  • If time expires, your turn is automatically forfeited
  • Timer pauses during piece flip animations

🏆 Strategy Tips

  • Corners are powerful - they can never be flipped!
  • Avoid squares next to corners - they give your opponent corner access
  • Control the center early in the game
  • Mobility matters - having more move options is often better than having more pieces
  • Edge control - controlling edges can lead to corner opportunities
  • Think ahead - consider what moves you're giving your opponent

🎯 Game End

  • Game ends when the board is completely full
  • Or when both players have no valid moves
  • Player with the most pieces wins
  • If equal pieces: it's a draw
