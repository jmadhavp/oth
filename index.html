Welcome to Othello!

🎮 How to Play

Othello is a strategic board game where you flip your opponent's pieces by sandwiching them between your pieces.

  • Place pieces to outflank opponent's pieces
  • Outflanked pieces flip to your color
  • Must flip at least one piece per move
  • Black (neon green) starts first

⏱️ Timer

You have 15 seconds per turn. If time runs out, your turn is forfeited.

🎯 Matchmaking

You'll be matched with opponents based on your skill rating. Win games to increase your rating!

💡 Pro Tips

  • Corner pieces are powerful - they can never be flipped!
  • Avoid placing pieces next to corners early in the game
  • Control the center for mobility

How to Play Othello

Objective

Have the most pieces of your color when the game ends.

Rules

  • Black (neon green) always starts first
  • Place your piece to sandwich opponent's pieces between your pieces (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally)
  • All sandwiched pieces flip to your color
  • You must flip at least one piece per move
  • If you have no valid moves, your turn is passed
  • Game ends when the board is full or both players have no valid moves

Strategy Tips

  • Corners are King: Corner pieces cannot be flipped, making them extremely valuable
  • Avoid Edge Traps: Placing pieces adjacent to empty corners can give your opponent the corner
  • Mobility Matters: Having more move options gives you an advantage
  • Control the Center: Early game, focus on controlling central squares
  • Think Ahead: Consider how your move sets up future turns

Timer

Each turn has a 15-second time limit. The timer changes color based on remaining time:

  • Green (15-10s): Plenty of time
  • Yellow (10-5s): Time is running low
  • Red (5-0s): Hurry up!

Matchmaking & Rating

Players are matched based on skill rating (starting at 1000). Win games to increase your rating and face tougher opponents!
